About the Lab

The Collective Memory and Dynamic Cognition Lab investigates how remembering is shaped by social interaction and how it changes over time. We combine behavioral experiments with quantitative and computational methods to study collaborative recall, the spread and correction of misinformation, and how group interaction influences what individuals go on to learn and remember. Undergraduate research assistants are core members of the lab, contributing to every stage of the research process — from study design to data collection and analysis.

Themes

Collaborative & Collective Memory

How do groups remember together, and what emerges from that process — shared understanding, converging error, or both? We study how interaction shapes what individuals and groups come to know and recall.

Dynamic Cognition

Memory and cognition are not static snapshots; they shift with context, time, and social input. We examine how remembering, learning, and belief change unfold dynamically across repeated exposure and interaction.

Misinformation & Knowledge Integrity

How does false information take hold in individual and collective memory, and what conditions make groups more or less resistant to it? This work connects basic memory research to real-world questions about trust and shared knowledge.
