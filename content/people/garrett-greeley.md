Garrett D. Greeley is a cognitive scientist studying how memory operates in social and dynamic contexts — how people construct, share, and revise knowledge together, and how those processes unfold over time. He directs the Collective Memory and Dynamic Cognition Lab, where he and his students investigate collaborative remembering, the spread (and correction) of misinformation, and how group interaction shapes individual learning.

He holds a Ph.D. in Cognitive Science from Stony Brook University. [Add current position / institution.]
