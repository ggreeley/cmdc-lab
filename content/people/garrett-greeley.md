Garrett D. Greeley is a cognitive scientist studying how memory operates in dynamic contexts: how people construct, share, and revise memories individually and in group settings, and how those processes unfold over time. He directs the Collective Memory and Dynamic Cognition Lab, where he and his students investigate memory as it is shaped by social interaction, temporal context, and the spread and correction of misinformation.

He holds a Ph.D. in Cognitive Science from Stony Brook University and is currently a Visiting Assistant Professor at Trinity University in San Antonio, TX.
