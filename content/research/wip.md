section: Work in Progress
---

- **Context Shift Mechanisms.** How does a stark context shift, induced by directed forgetting instructions, interfere with context reinstatement? We're examining this question at multiple levels of analysis, including potential breakdowns in temporal context reconstruction using fine-grained analyses (e.g., Lag-CRP). Phase: Data Collection
- **Cue Strategy as Support or Hindrance.** How does the *serial position* of words at encoding influence the extent to which they support or hinder recall when used as cues during retrieval? We're addressing this question by integrating classic work on part-set cuing effects with modern theoretical ideas about context maintenance and retrieval. Phase: Data Collection
- **Quantifying Collective Memory in Larger Groups.** A strict definition of collective memory requires that all members of a group recall a particular word or detail. In real life, that bar is too high. We are developing metrics for characterizing collective memory in larger groups and networks that take a *spectrum* approach instead of an all-or-nothing one. Phase: In Development
