## Dynamic Cognition
Memory and cognition are not static snapshots; they shift with context, time, and experience. We examine how remembering, learning, and belief change unfold dynamically as people navigate new information and changing circumstances.

## Collaborative & Collective Memory
Social interaction is one powerful context that shapes memory. How do groups remember together, and what emerges from that process: shared understanding, converging error, or both? We study how interaction shapes what individuals and groups come to know and recall.

## Misinformation & Knowledge Integrity
How does false information take hold in individual and collective memory, and what conditions make groups more or less resistant to it? This work connects basic memory research to real-world questions about trust and shared knowledge.
